# Stackivo — Go-To-Market Playbook (LinkedIn · Instagram · Reddit)

**Goal (next 30–60 days):** first 20–50 real, active users + feedback.
**Audience:** Indian freelancers (solo consultants, designers, developers, writers, etc.) who invoice clients, deal with GST, and struggle to get paid on time.
**Effort:** heavy (10+ hrs/week).
**Core assets:** founder story/POV · product screenshots + demos · the free calculators (GST, late-payment interest, freelance rate).

---

## 1. The core strategy (read this first)

Everything hangs on three ideas:

**a) The free tools are the wedge, not the product.**
Your calculators are the single most ban-resistant, share-friendly thing you own. A "GST calculator" or "late-payment interest calculator" is *genuinely helpful* — communities welcome it where they'd remove a signup link. Lead with the tool, let the product be discovered second.

**b) Founder-as-the-face beats brand-as-broadcaster.**
"I'm a freelancer/developer building the invoicing tool I wished I had" outperforms a faceless company account on every one of these platforms — especially early. People follow people and root for builders.

**c) The funnel is: help → trust → soft mention → signup for feedback.**
You are not "selling." You're solving a specific painful problem in public, and inviting people to try the thing that solves it and tell you what's wrong with it. That framing ("I'd love 20 freelancers to break this and tell me what sucks") converts *and* protects you from spam flags.

**Positioning line to reuse everywhere:**
> Stackivo is the all-in-one workspace for Indian freelancers — GST-ready invoices, contracts, client portals, and getting paid — without the spreadsheet chaos.

---

## 2. The golden rules — never get banned or shadowbanned

These apply across all three platforms:

1. **One real human account per platform. Never automate.** No bots, no follow/unfollow tools, no mass-DM tools, no auto-commenters. Automation is the #1 cause of permanent bans on LinkedIn and Instagram. Everything you do is manual.
2. **Never post the identical link/text across many places in a short window.** Platform spam filters detect repetition. Rewrite every time; vary the angle.
3. **Follow the 9:1 rule religiously (especially Reddit).** For every 1 post that mentions your product, do ~9 posts/comments that are pure value with zero self-interest.
4. **Lead with the free tool or a genuine insight, not the signup page.** A link to a calculator or guide is "helpful." A link to "sign up for my SaaS" is "spam." Same product, wildly different reception.
5. **Read the rules of every community before posting.** Subreddits, LinkedIn groups, and IG's community guidelines all differ. 30 seconds of reading saves an account.
6. **Never buy followers, never run engagement pods, never ask for upvotes.** Vote manipulation and fake engagement are bannable and shadowban-triggering.
7. **Warm up new accounts.** A brand-new account that immediately posts links looks like spam to the algorithm. Comment and participate for 1–2 weeks first.
8. **Space out your actions.** Don't send 50 connection requests or 30 DMs in an hour. Human pace only.
9. **Assume every numeric limit is lower than you think and changes often.** Stay well under. When unsure, do less.

## 3. Platform playbooks

### 🔵 LinkedIn — your #1 channel (highest intent, B2B, active Indian freelancer scene)

**Why it's first:** Indian freelancers, consultants, and small-agency owners are genuinely active here, the audience is in "work mode," and founder build-in-public content performs very well right now.

**Content pillars (rotate these):**
- **Pain stories** — the late-paying client, the GST filing panic, the contract that wasn't signed and cost you. Emotionally resonant, endlessly relatable.
- **Build-in-public** — what you're building, why, decisions, screenshots, small wins ("shipped GST-ready invoices today"), honest struggles. This *is* your founder-story asset.
- **Tactical value** — "How to set your freelance rate," "What every freelance contract needs," "How GST actually works for freelancers." (Repurpose the calculators here.)
- **Product-as-solution** — a screenshot of Stackivo solving the exact pain from a story post. Show, don't pitch.

**Cadence (heavy):** 4–5 posts/week + comment thoughtfully on 5–10 other people's posts daily. *Commenting is half your growth — it's how new people discover you.*

**Formats that work:** short text posts (hook + story + lesson), PDF/image carousels (guides), single screenshots with a story, occasional short native video.

**Anti-ban / reach rules specific to LinkedIn:**
- **Put external links in the FIRST COMMENT, not the post body.** LinkedIn suppresses reach on posts with outbound links. Post the content natively, drop the Stackivo/calculator link as your own first comment, and say "link in comments."
- **Personalize every connection request; keep the volume modest and human-paced.** Mass or templated invites get your invites restricted. Aim for quality connections with your target audience, not volume.
- **No third-party automation (Dux-Soup, etc.).** LinkedIn actively detects and bans these.
- **DMs must be 1:1 and personal.** Never paste the same pitch to 40 people. A good DM references something specific about them.

**First move:** rewrite your personal profile as a landing page — headline that says what you do for freelancers, an "about" that tells the founder story, and a featured link to a free calculator.

### 📸 Instagram — reach + relatability engine (younger freelancers, top-of-funnel)

**Why it matters:** Reels are the best free organic reach on the internet right now, and younger Indian freelancers/creators live here. It's more top-of-funnel than LinkedIn — awareness and vibe, converting via link-in-bio.

**Content pillars:**
- **Quick tips (Reels)** — "3 things missing from your freelance invoice," "The late-fee clause that gets you paid," delivered in 15–30s.
- **Free-tool demos (Reels)** — screen-record the GST/rate calculator solving a real question. Highly saveable/shareable.
- **Relatable freelance-life content** — the "client says 'final revision' for the 6th time" energy. Memes/relatable Reels get shared and shared = reach.
- **Carousel guides** — GST for freelancers, rate-setting, contract checklist. Educational carousels get saved (saves = algorithm gold).
- **Behind-the-scenes** — building Stackivo, your desk, your process (founder story, humanized).

**Cadence (heavy):** 4–5 Reels/week + daily Stories. Reels are the growth driver; carousels build authority; Stories nurture the people who already follow.

**Anti-ban / shadowban rules specific to Instagram:**
- **Links aren't clickable in captions** — drive everything to **link-in-bio** (use a simple bio-link page pointing to your free tools + signup). Never spam a URL in captions hoping it works.
- **No follow/unfollow churn, no DM automation, no bought followers.** These are the fastest routes to a shadowban.
- **Use a moderate, relevant, *rotating* hashtag set (~5–15), and avoid banned/spammy tags.** Repeating the exact same big block of hashtags on every post can look botlike — mix niche + medium tags, vary them.
- **Schedule only with Meta's own tools** (Business Suite) or post natively. Sketchy third-party schedulers can trip automation flags.
- **Don't post the exact same clip repeatedly.** Re-cut/re-caption.

**First move:** convert to a Business/Creator account, write a bio that names the audience + benefit, set up the bio-link, and prep a batch of 8–10 Reels before you start (so heavy cadence is sustainable).

### 🟠 Reddit — highest-intent, highest-risk (participation, not promotion)

**Why it's powerful *and* dangerous:** freelancers literally ask "how do I invoice with GST / get a client to pay / set my rate" on Reddit. If you're the helpful person who answers, it converts beautifully. If you show up cold with a product link, you get removed, downvoted, and possibly shadowbanned — and Reddit shadowbans are silent (your posts are invisible and you won't know).

**Mandatory warm-up (weeks 1–2): do NOT post about Stackivo yet.**
- Use one genuine account with your real interests.
- Spend the first 1–2 weeks *only commenting helpfully* in your target subs to build karma and account history. Answer freelance/GST/getting-paid questions with genuinely useful replies — no links.
- Many subs auto-remove posts/links from low-karma or new accounts. Karma is your entry ticket.

**How to actually use Reddit (after warm-up):**
- **Be the answer.** Search each sub for people asking about invoicing, late payments, GST, rate-setting. Write the best, most complete answer. *Only if directly relevant*, mention "I built a free [GST/late-fee] calculator that does this — [link]" — tool first, never the signup page.
- **Follow the 9:1 rule hard.** ~9 pure-help comments for every 1 self-referential mention.
- **Respect each sub's self-promo rules.** Some ban promotion entirely; some have a weekly "share your project" thread; some (like r/SideProject) welcome Show-and-tell. Read the sidebar/rules first, every time.
- **Never cross-post the same thing to 5 subs.** That's the classic spam pattern. One tailored post per sub, spaced out.
- **Never use alt accounts to upvote yourself.** Vote manipulation = instant ban.
- **A "Show" post done right:** in a sub that allows it, post as a builder — "I'm a freelancer who got tired of X, so I built Y, it's free to try, brutal feedback welcome." Humble, feedback-seeking, no hard sell.

**Starter subreddits to research (verify rules + activity before posting):**
`r/developersIndia`, `r/IndianFreelancers` / `r/freelanceIndia`, `r/india`, `r/IndiaTax` (GST-relevant), `r/smallbusinessindia`, `r/IndianStartups`, plus global: `r/freelance`, `r/SideProject`, `r/juststart`, `r/Entrepreneur`, `r/smallbusiness`. Confirm each is active and read its promo policy first.

**How to detect a shadowban:** log out (or use a private window) and check whether your posts/comments are visible. If only you can see them, you're shadowbanned — stop, and rebuild trust slowly.

## 4. The content repurposing engine (so heavy cadence is sustainable)

One idea → all three platforms. This is how you post daily without burning out:

1. **Start with a pain point or insight** (e.g., "freelancers lose money by not adding a late-payment clause").
2. **LinkedIn:** write it as a story post (+ calculator link in first comment).
3. **Instagram:** cut it into a 20–30s Reel *and* a carousel.
4. **Reddit:** keep it in your back pocket as the perfect *answer* when someone asks a related question — reworded, native, helpful.
5. **Free tool tie-in:** if the idea maps to a calculator, that calculator is the CTA everywhere.

Batch weekly: pick 3–4 core ideas on Monday, produce all formats, schedule/queue, then spend the rest of the week engaging (comments, DMs, Reddit answers) — engagement is where the growth actually happens.

## 5. First 14 days — concrete launch sequence

**Days 1–3 — Foundations (no promotion yet):**
- Optimize all three profiles (bio, founder story, bio-link, featured calculator).
- Set up the bio-link page (free tools + signup).
- Start Reddit warm-up: 3–5 genuine helpful comments/day, zero links.
- Batch-produce your first week of content.

**Days 4–7 — Turn on LinkedIn + Instagram:**
- LinkedIn: post your founder-origin story ("why I'm building Stackivo"). Begin daily commenting.
- Instagram: publish first 2–3 Reels + first carousel. Daily Stories.
- Reddit: keep warming up (still no promotion).

**Days 8–14 — Add value + first soft product mentions:**
- LinkedIn: 4–5 posts (mix pain stories + a tactical guide + one product screenshot). Start personalized 1:1 DMs to freelancers who engage.
- Instagram: 4–5 Reels + 1–2 carousels. Reply to every comment/DM.
- Reddit: begin answering real questions; drop free-calculator links *only where directly helpful*. Consider one Show-and-tell in a promo-friendly sub if karma allows.
- **Direct ask everywhere:** "Looking for 20 Indian freelancers to try Stackivo free and tell me what's broken — DM me." High-intent, ban-safe, feedback-oriented.

## 7. What to track (keep it simple)

- **North-star:** signups + activated users (someone who created an invoice/used a feature).
- **Leading indicators:** profile visits, bio-link clicks, calculator uses, DMs started, "how do I try it" replies.
- **Per platform:** which pillar/format drove the most saves/comments/clicks — double down on winners.
- **Qualitative:** every piece of feedback from your first users. At this stage, *conversations > vanity metrics.*

---

## 8. Hard "don'ts" (pin this)

- ❌ No automation/bots on any platform.
- ❌ No identical copy-paste across subs/DMs/posts.
- ❌ No signup-link-first posting — free tool or insight first, always.
- ❌ No buying followers, no upvote asks, no engagement pods.
- ❌ No promoting on Reddit before warming up the account.
- ❌ No arguing with critics publicly — take feedback graciously (it builds trust).

---

*Note on limits:* exact numeric thresholds (connection requests/week, IG action limits, karma gates) change frequently and aren't published precisely by the platforms. The safe policy is to always stay conservative and human-paced — the behaviors above matter far more than any specific number. When in doubt, do less and be more genuine.
