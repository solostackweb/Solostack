# Stackivo — LinkedIn Content Engine (Batch 1)

Personal-profile, one post/day, founder-led. Every post = **copy + image + first-comment link**.
This doc gives you: (1) a reusable visual system for Lovable, (2) the posting rules, (3) the 14-day arc, (4) the first 3 days fully scripted.

---

## 0. How to use this

1. Copy the **Master Visual System** block (Section 1) into every Lovable prompt, then paste the day's specific **Design Prompt** under it.
2. Upload your real logo (`logo-assets/stackivo-wordmark.svg` / `stackivo-icon.svg`) into Lovable so the mark is exact — don't let AI redraw it.
3. Post the copy natively. **Put the link in the FIRST COMMENT**, not the post body (LinkedIn suppresses posts with outbound links).
4. Post at a consistent time — for Indian freelancers, **8–10 AM IST** or **7–9 PM IST** tend to work. Pick one and keep it.
5. Reply to every comment in the first 60–90 minutes (early engagement decides reach).

## 2. Posting rules (pin these)

- **Hook first.** The first 1–2 lines must earn the "see more" click. No throat-clearing.
- **Short lines, whitespace.** Mobile-first. One idea per line, blank lines between beats.
- **One CTA, soft.** Usually: "I'm looking for freelancers to try it and tell me what's broken."
- **Link in first comment**, and say "(link in comments)".
- **3–5 relevant hashtags** max, at the end: rotate from `#freelancing #freelanceindia #freelancer #gst #invoicing #buildinpublic #indianfreelancers #solopreneur #freelancelife`.
- **No engagement-bait** ("comment YES"), no automation, no mass tagging. (See GTM playbook §2.)
- **Tag sparingly & only relevantly** — 0–2 genuinely relevant people, never random.

## 5. Reusable copy frameworks (for when you write your own)

- **Pain → lesson → line:** open with a specific painful moment → the lesson → the exact fix (a clause, a habit, a number).
- **Story → build:** a real freelΓancing moment → "so I'm building X for it."
- **Free tool hook:** a question with a number → "most people don't know" → "I built a free thing" → soft product tie-in.
- **Build-in-public:** "This week I shipped ___ / decided ___ / struggled with ___" + screenshot + what you learned.

**Hook templates that pull the "see more":**
- "A client once paid me 71 days late."
- "I spent 3 years freelancing. I was terrible at [X]."
- "₹[amount] invoice. [N] days late. What do they owe you now?"
- "Nobody warns you about GST when you start freelancing."
- "I almost quit freelancing over [X]. Here's what fixed it."
