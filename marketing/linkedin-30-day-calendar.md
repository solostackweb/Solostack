## DAY 16 — How-to: invoicing international clients

**COPY:**
```
Invoicing your first international client from India? Quick starter (not tax advice):

1. Agree the currency up front — usually USD/EUR. Put it in writing.
2. Your invoice can often be zero-rated GST (export of services) — but you still report it. Keep your paperwork clean (LUT if applicable).
3. Show your details clearly: PAN, address, and the client's country.
4. Pick the payment rail before you invoice — Wise, Payoneer, SWIFT — and state fees/who bears them.
5. Set expectations: international payments take longer. Add terms + a follow-up plan.
6. Track the FX — the ₹ you invoice and the ₹ you receive won't match. Record the real amount.

The admin is annoying, but "I work with international clients" is a great position to be in.

Stackivo handles the INR + foreign-currency flows so you don't run two systems.
```
