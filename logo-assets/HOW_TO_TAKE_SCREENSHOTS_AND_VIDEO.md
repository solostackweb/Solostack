## DEMO VIDEO — Using Loom (free, takes 10 minutes)

### Step 1: Install Loom (completely free)
1. Go to **loom.com**
2. Sign up with your Google account (free)
3. Install the Chrome extension OR download the desktop app
4. It adds a small button to your browser

### Step 2: What to record (2-minute video)

**The script — say exactly this while recording:**

---
*[Open the app at the Dashboard]*

**"Hi, I'm [your name], the founder of Stackivo — a business OS built specifically for Indian freelancers."**

*[Pause 1 second]*

**"Let me show you how it works in under 2 minutes."**

*[Click to Clients]*

**"You start by adding your clients here — name, company, email, everything in one place."**

*[Click to Invoices → Create Invoice]*

**"Then you create a GST-compliant invoice — Stackivo automatically handles CGST, SGST, and IGST. You pick the client, add line items, and it calculates everything."**

*[Show the GST breakdown and total]*

**"When you're ready, you hit send — and your client gets a payment link where they can pay via UPI, card, or netbanking directly. No manual Razorpay link creation needed."**

*[Click to Contracts]*

**"You can also send contracts for your clients to sign digitally — draw signature, type it, or upload. Everything has a legal audit trail."**

*[Click to Pulse]*

**"And Pulse shows you your revenue, pending payments, and business health at a glance."**

*[Back to Dashboard]*

**"Stackivo replaces Excel, WhatsApp screenshots, Google Drive, and manual UPI links — in one ₹499/month workspace. Free plan available at stackivo.me."**

---

### Step 3: How to record
1. Open Stackivo in Chrome at full screen
2. Click the Loom button in Chrome toolbar
3. Select "Screen + Camera" (or just "Screen" if you don't want your face)
4. Select "Current Tab" 
5. Click "Start Recording"
6. Follow the script above — speak clearly, move slowly
7. Click Stop when done
8. Loom gives you a link AND a download option — **download the MP4**
9. Save as: `stackivo-demo-video.mp4`

### Tips for a good recording:
- Close all other tabs before recording
- Turn off notifications (Do Not Disturb mode)
- Speak slowly — most people rush
- If you make a mistake, just pause, take a breath, and continue — you can trim in Loom
- Don't worry about being perfect — authentic founder videos outperform polished agency videos
