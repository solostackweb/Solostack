# Design System — Stackivo

`design-system/MASTER.md` is the detailed and authoritative specification. This
file is the fast orientation layer required before any visual work.

## Product context

- **Product:** A business operating system connecting clients, projects,
  contracts, invoices, GST, payments, files, portals and Ivo workflows.
- **Audience:** Indian freelancers and small studios.
- **Surfaces:** Marketing, auth, onboarding, workspace, public documents,
  client portals, support and admin.

## Direction

- **Name:** Calm Command
- **Memorable impression:** Calm, unmistakably blue, serious software that does
  not look machine-generated.
- **Reference:** [Picktime — Smart Meetings Platform](https://dribbble.com/shots/25267926-Picktime-Smart-Meetings-Platform-SAAS), used for atmosphere and composition only.
- **Signature:** The Flowline connects real client-work artifacts across
  marketing and product.

## Core system

- **Primary:** `#2563EB`
- **Deep blue:** `#173EA5`
- **Periwinkle:** `#7186E8`
- **Canvas:** `#F7F9FC`
- **Ink:** `#111936`
- **Border:** `#DCE2EE`
- **Display:** Plus Jakarta Sans Variable
- **Body/UI:** Instrument Sans Variable
- **Data:** IBM Plex Mono
- **Spacing:** 4px base; spacious marketing, comfortable product, compact operations
- **Layout:** Editorial 12-column marketing; disciplined product grid
- **Motion:** Intentional, finite, transform/opacity only
- **Radius:** 6 / 10 / 16 / 24 / full, each with a defined purpose

## Non-negotiables

- Semantic colours only in components
- Flat primary buttons, never gradient CTAs
- Real product artifacts and credible data
- No repetitive generic feature grids
- No invented proof or unsupported product claims
- WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, 44px targets and reduced-motion support
- Marketing light-only; workspace light-default with a designed dark mode
